Features

One record for each operation

01

Inventory with history

Register products and stock movements with quantities, reasons and an audit trail.

02

Purchasing and receiving

Record supplier orders and confirm physical receipts, including partial deliveries.

03

Sales and receipts

Confirm stock, payment and sale together. Review and print the original receipt from sales history.

04

Dispatch coordination

Group sales into a manifest, assign a driver and vehicle, and track delivery status.

A shared workflow

  1. Receive goods from a supplier and record the stock entry.
  2. Confirm a sale and consult its receipt with date, location and item details.
  3. Assign the sale to a dispatch and record delivery progress.

For business owners

Check daily sales and low stock, and create operational accounts for your team.

For warehouse staff

Use clear forms for receipts, stock exits and product history.
